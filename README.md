## For why?
Though this may be an unpopular opinion, I am not a recipe book hater. In concept, it's a great interface that I prefer to the bulkiness of recipe browser mods.

But in practice, it's missing a lot of necessary functionality (especially for modded playthroughs) that recipe browsers bring to the table. That's what this mod aims to fix.

## Features
When used **server-side**:
- **No More Recipe Unlocks**
  - Completely removes recipe book NBT and recipe unlocking.
  - Still handles recipes for players on vanilla clients by treating them as all unlocked.

When used **client-side**, with [**JEI**](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei), [**REI**](https://modrinth.com/mod/rei), or [**EMI**](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi) installed:
- **Recipe Browser Integration**
  - The recipe book now populates its entries from your choice favorite recipe browser.
- **Mod Support**
  - Recipe categories are now populated from item groups (creative tabs), which provides a more organized layout, especially for modded recipes.
  - Works with most mods with recipe browser support.
- **No More Browser**
  - Permanently hides the recipe browser interface while keeping other functionality of the mod intact.
  - Generic item information (e.g. items not obtained through standard crafting) can be viewed through the recipe book in your inventory.


### Alternatives
If you are just looking to remove the recipe book entirely, I recommend checking out [**Not Enough Recipe Book**](https://modrinth.com/mod/notenoughrecipebook).
